Questions

Questions about working with us.

Who would come to the park?

Aun Naeem, founding director and themed designer. He leads the site visit and the work that follows. The rest of the team is named in the proposal.

Are you insured?

Professional indemnity and public liability are confirmed in the proposal. Certificates are available on request.

What would you see that our own data does not?

Ticket data, queue systems and guest surveys stay with you. The review sets them beside a walk of the site, and separates what operations can change this month from what needs design or a survey. The measures are the ones a board already uses: throughput, dwell, spend per guest, satisfaction and maintenance.

What happens on a site walk?

We walk the site with you, agree the problem, and set out the work from there: design, planning, repair, construction and theming.

We already survey our guests.

Good. Surveys tell you what guests say. We use them with time on site, and separate operational changes from design work.

Where should an engagement begin?

With the site and the problem. The same project can cover design, planning, repair, construction, making and theming.

Our team knows the site better than anyone.

They do. We work with them. An external review sees what becomes difficult to see in daily operation.

Can you obtain planning permission?

Yes. We prepare the application and take it through to a decision, as part of the project.

How is success defined?

The measures are agreed at the start and written into the proposal.

Do we have to close anything while you work?

No. Surveys are arranged outside operating hours, observation happens during normal operation, and building work is phased around your season.

When is the best time to talk to you?

Autumn and winter, while next season is being planned and budgeted. We also work through the season on observation and quick fixes.

Where are you based?

The studio is at 66 Paul Street, London EC2A 4NA, United Kingdom. We work on sites across the United Kingdom and further afield.

Do you work outside the United Kingdom?

Yes. Projects are agreed around the site, not around our office.

Who owns the work?

Ownership of reports and drawings is set out in the written agreement for that engagement. Nothing on this website is itself a contract.

How quickly do you reply?

We reply within one working day.

Still something to ask? Contact the studio.
