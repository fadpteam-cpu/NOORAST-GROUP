Accessibility Reviews

A design review of how easily every guest can arrive, move around and take part.

Contact

About

Access affects wheelchair users, families with buggies, older visitors and people with sensory or neurodivergent needs. We follow the routes guests take, from parking to the exit, and identify what makes the visit harder for any guest. Recommendations are set out in order, from simple changes to design work. This is a design review, not a certified access audit. Where a formal audit is needed, we will say so and can bring in a registered access consultant.

When it applies

  • Guests have raised questions or complaints about access
  • Routes, entrances or toilets have not been looked at in recent years
  • Works are planned and access should be designed in from the start

If it is leftGuests who cannot fully take part in the visit, and fewer return visits from them and the people they come with.

What we do

  • Arrival, parking, drop-off and entrances
  • Routes, gradients, surfaces and resting points
  • Toilets, changing places and baby change
  • Queue lines, viewing areas, food and retail
  • Signage, wayfinding and quiet spaces
  • Access information published online

What you receive

  • Review report with photographs
  • Recommendations, from simple changes to design work
  • Draft access information for your website

Programme

  1. Week 1Brief, site information and guest feedback on access
  2. Weeks 1 to 2Review on site, following the routes guests take
  3. Weeks 3 to 6Report, design recommendations and visitor access information
Best for
Any attraction open to the public, and any operator planning works on its site.
Duration
3 to 6 weeks
Measured by
Barriers identified and designed out, and clear access information for visitors
