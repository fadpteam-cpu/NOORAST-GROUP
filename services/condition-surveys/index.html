Building and Condition Surveys

A clear record of what is on site, its condition, and what needs attention first.

Contact

About

Buildings, themed structures, shelters, queue lines and paths all age, and many parks do not have accurate drawings of what they own. We survey what is there, record its condition, and set out repairs in order of priority, so maintenance can be planned before the season rather than during it.

When it applies

  • There are no reliable drawings of the site
  • Theming, shelters or buildings are showing their age
  • Repairs are dealt with as they arise, not planned

If it is leftUnplanned repairs, and areas closed during the season.

What we do

  • Measured survey of buildings and themed structures
  • Condition survey of buildings, theming, shelters, queue structures and paths
  • Photographic record
  • Repairs set out by priority, with indicative budgets
  • Items to address before the next season

What you receive

  • Survey drawings
  • Condition report
  • Repair and maintenance schedule

Programme

  1. Week 1Brief, drawings request and access arrangements
  2. Weeks 1 to 3Survey on site, arranged outside operating hours
  3. Weeks 3 to 6Survey drawings, condition report and repair schedule
Best for
Operators planning maintenance budgets, refurbishment or new work on an existing site.
Duration
3 to 6 weeks
Measured by
A prioritised, budgeted repair plan and accurate drawings of the site
