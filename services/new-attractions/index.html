Services

New Attractions and Expansions

Ride buildings, themed lands, queue buildings and entrances, designed around how guests will use them.

Contact

About

We design the buildings and places around a new attraction: the ride or show building, its queue, the land it sits in, and the entrance, food and retail around it. The guest journey and capacity are tested before the design is fixed, so the attraction works on its busiest day as well as its opening day.

When it applies

  • A new ride, land or building is being planned
  • The ride supplier needs a building, queue and setting designed around it
  • Capacity and guest flow have not yet been tested

If it is leftProblems with flow and capacity are costly to change once an attraction has opened.

What we do

  • Feasibility and site review
  • Story, theme and concept design
  • Guest journey, queue and capacity design
  • Coordination with ride suppliers and engineers
  • Planning application, where required
  • Technical design and building regulations

What you receive

  • Concept design with visuals
  • Planning drawings, where required
  • Technical design package

Programme

  1. Stage 1Brief, site review and feasibility
  2. Stage 2Concept design, guest journey and capacity
  3. Stage 3Planning application, where required
  4. Stage 4Technical design and a package for construction
Best for
Operators, developers and landowners planning a new attraction or a major addition.
Duration
4 to 12 months, by stage
Measured by
An attraction that opens on time, works at capacity and suits the site
