Guest Experience Review

An independent review of the whole visit, with the actions set out in order.

Contact

About

We spend two to three days on site, including a busy day. We time queues, watch how guests move, and read the park’s surveys and reviews. The report separates changes the operations team can make from work that needs design.

When it applies

  • Queues dominate guest feedback
  • Value for money or service scores are weak
  • Scores are moving and teams disagree on the reason

If it is leftBudget is spent on the wrong change while the real cause remains.

What we do

  • Two to three days on site, including a peak day
  • Mystery visits by guest type: young families, teenagers and older visitors
  • Journey mapping from booking to departure
  • Queue timing and observation of guest flow
  • Review of the offer: tickets, passes, food and paid extras
  • Service observation and conversations with frontline teams
  • Analysis of 12 to 24 months of online reviews

What you receive

  • Review report
  • Ordered action plan, separating operational changes from design work
  • Guest experience scorecard to measure against
  • Findings session with leadership

Programme

  1. Week 1Brief, data request and review analysis
  2. Weeks 1 to 2Two to three days on site, including a peak day
  3. Weeks 3 to 4Analysis, scorecard and action plan
  4. Week 5Findings session with your leadership team
Best for
Operators who want a clear, independent account of the visit before committing to wider work.
Duration
3 to 5 weeks
Measured by
A measured baseline, the principal issues in order, and an agreed plan
