Masterplanning and Planning Applications

A long-term plan for the site, and the planning permission to deliver it.

Contact

About

Many attractions have grown one project at a time. A masterplan sets out where new attractions, facilities and routes should go over the next ten years, and in what order. Where new work needs planning permission, we prepare the application and manage it through to a decision.

When it applies

  • The site has grown without a long-term plan
  • A new structure may need planning permission
  • The site has constraints: green belt, heritage, neighbours or noise

If it is leftPiecemeal additions that do not fit together, and delays while permission is sought.

What we do

  • Site analysis and planning history
  • Masterplan options, phasing and guest routes
  • Pre-application discussions with the council
  • Planning drawings and design and access statements
  • Coordination of specialist reports, such as noise and transport
  • Submission and management of the application

What you receive

  • Masterplan and phasing plan
  • Planning application
  • Responses to council queries through to a decision

Programme

  1. Weeks 1 to 4Site analysis, constraints and planning history
  2. Weeks 5 to 10Masterplan options and phasing
  3. Weeks 11 to 14Pre-application advice with the council, where useful
  4. FollowingApplication submitted and managed to a decision
Best for
Operators and landowners planning growth, and anyone with new work that needs permission.
Duration
3 to 4 months, plus the planning period
Measured by
A plan the business can invest against, and permissions secured
