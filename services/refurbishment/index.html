Refurbishment and Retheming

Existing areas, queues and buildings improved without closing the attraction.

Contact

About

Theming wears, queues age and some areas are passed through rather than used. We refurbish and retheme spaces that remain in operation: queue environments, entrances, plazas, food and retail units, and whole areas. Work is phased around the season, so the site stays open.

When it applies

  • An area of the site is under-used
  • Theming has worn since it opened
  • A queue, entrance or plaza needs redesigning, not just repainting

If it is leftSpace that is maintained but little used, and one less reason for guests to return.

What we do

  • Condition review of the areas in question
  • Story and theme
  • Queue environments: shade, seating, sightlines and information
  • Materials, landscape, light, sound and graphics
  • Seasonal overlays for events
  • Phasing and cost planning around live operation

What you receive

  • Concept package with visuals
  • Drawings, specification and cost plan
  • Delivery support with fabrication partners, scoped separately

Programme

  1. Weeks 1 to 3Site walk, condition review and brief
  2. Weeks 4 to 8Story, theme and concept design
  3. Weeks 9 to 14Drawings, visuals, specification and cost plan
  4. Weeks 15 to 20Phasing around the season and delivery support
Best for
Operators with dated areas, tired queues or under-used parts of the site.
Duration
8 to 20 weeks
Measured by
Dwell time, satisfaction with the area and time spent in queues
