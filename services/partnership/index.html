Ongoing Partnership

Retained support across guest experience, design and the condition of the site.

About

Regular measurement, time on site, design advice and an annual survey update, so improvements are maintained and the next projects are planned in good time.

When it applies

  • A first project worked and the momentum should continue
  • An outside view each season would be useful
  • Maintenance and improvement need planning a year ahead

If it is leftWithout regular attention, the gains from a first project fade within a season or two.

What we do

  • Monthly review of guest feedback
  • Regular site visits and scorecard updates
  • Design advice within agreed days
  • Annual condition survey update
  • Seasonal planning and an annual strategy review
