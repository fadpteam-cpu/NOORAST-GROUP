Sectors

Family entertainment and indoor play

Indoor play and family entertainment centres depend on people coming back. Layout, theming and consistency matter more than a single new ride.

The spaces wear quickly. Guests notice when a queue, a party room or a route no longer works.

Refurbishment, phased around opening hours, is the usual commission.
